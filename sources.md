# Sources and indicator dictionary

Primary data source: World Bank Open Data API, using annual country indicator endpoints. API documentation: https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898599-indicator-api-queries

| Display label | World Bank code | Units / interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Life expectancy at birth | `SP.DYN.LE00.IN` | Years; total population |
| Unemployment rate (national estimate) | `SL.UEM.TOTL.NE.ZS` | % of total labor force; national estimate |
| GDP per capita | `NY.GDP.PCAP.CD` | Current US$ per person; not household income or average wealth |
| Crude birth rate | `SP.DYN.CBRT.IN` | Births per 1,000 people; not a total birth count |
| Net migration | `SM.POP.NETM` | People; net migration, not an emigration count |
| Intentional homicide rate | `VC.IHR.PSRC.P5` | Intentional homicides per 100,000 people |
| Consumer price inflation | `FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG` | Annual % change |
| Central government debt | `GC.DOD.TOTL.GD.ZS` | % of GDP; not all public-sector debt |
| Total reserves, including gold | `FI.RES.TOTL.CD` | Current US$ |
| External balance: goods and services | `NE.RSB.GNFS.ZS` | % of GDP; not the current account |

Indicator pages use the pattern `https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/CODE`. The app labels the source and retrieval date in the interface and report.

## Context sources to consult for the written research brief
- World Bank country and indicator pages: https://data.worldbank.org/
- United Nations Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean (ECLAC): https://www.cepal.org/en
- Ecuador's National Institute of Statistics and Census (INEC): https://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/
- Colombia's National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE): https://www.dane.gov.co/

For the final brief, choose and cite two specific historical/cultural/context pages or reports from ECLAC, INEC, DANE, or another credible source. Do not claim a causal explanation unless the source supports it.
